# Prime
## 📊 Prime Video Content Analytics Dashboard
<img width="1310" height="730" alt="PrimeDashboard" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a347007d-ee4b-4879-aab5-4adce9dda306" />

This is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that explores Amazon Prime Video content. It lets you dig into trends, genres, ratings, countries, and show titles in a visual way — great for understanding content patterns and sharing insights.

---

##  What This Dashboard Does

The report lets you:

- See how many shows Prime Video released each year
- Compare **Movies vs. TV Shows**
- Explore which **genres** are most common
- Check age **rating distribution**
- View content **by country** on a map
- Browse specific titles in a scrollable list
- Slice and filter by year and genre

Everything updates interactively so you can explore the data from different angles.

---

## 📊 Main Sections

### **📈 Trends Over Time**
Line chart showing the total number of shows released each year — split by movies and TV shows.

### **🎭 Genre Breakdown**
Bar chart showing the most common genres (Drama, Comedy, etc.).

### **⭐ Ratings Distribution**
Shows how many titles fall into each rating category (like 13+, R, PG-13).

### **🌍 Map of Shows by Country**
A map highlighting countries with Prime Video content.

### **🔎 Title List**
Scrollable table showing show titles. Use slicers to narrow it down (e.g., by genre or year).

### **🧰 Filters**
Interactive filters for:
- Release Year
- Genre

These update all visuals on the page.

---

## 📌 How to Use

1. Download and open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.
2. Use the filters on the right to narrow the data.
3. Click on visuals and titles to explore relationships.
4. Hover over charts and map points for tooltips and details.

---

## 📁 About the Data

Fields used include:
- **title**: Show name
- **release_year**: Year the show came out
- **listed_in**: Genre(s)
- **rating**: Age rating
- **country**: Country of origin
- **type**: Movie or TV Show

The dataset includes around 9,600 entries with a mix of movies and TV shows from various regions.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways (Examples)

- Drama is the most frequent genre.
- Most titles are TV shows.
- Prime Video content releases climbed significantly after 2015.
- Some countries have much more content than others.
